## Design Context

### Users

MIC investors and portfolio reviewers using the investor portal to monitor holdings, payment health, concentration, and maturity exposure—often on desktop, sometimes on tablet, expecting institutional clarity without generic “admin template” visuals.

### Brand Personality

**Grounded, exacting, quietly confident** — numbers-first, regulator-aware, FairLend lagoon/sea palette as the thread (not decoration).

### Aesthetic Direction

Refined **institutional dark-first** (portal already runs in dark context); **lagoon-tinted neutrals** and existing `--lagoon` / `--sea-ink` tokens; **editorial hierarchy** (type scale and spacing, not card stacks). **Anti-references:** lazy card grids, heavy internal grid borders, cyan/purple gradients, gradient text, thick left accent stripes.

### Design Principles

1. **Hierarchy through typography and rhythm**, not boxes—banded sections and whitespace over repeated bordered cards.
2. **Charts only where the data series is explicit** (e.g. maturity ladder histogram)—no decorative sparklines.
3. **OKLCH and `color-mix`** for surfaces and borders; no pure `#000` / `#fff` fills for large areas.
4. **Dense, honest tables** for positions, payments, and histories; timelines as slim rows, not card carousels.
5. **Touch parity:** long-press where hover reveals detail (histogram tooltips).
